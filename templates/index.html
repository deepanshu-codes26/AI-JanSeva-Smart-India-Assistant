HELP CENTER

Frequently asked
questions.

A few important things to know before you begin.

Is this an official government website?

No. AI JanSeva is an educational citizen-assistance prototype, not a government department or official portal.

Does this website confirm my eligibility?

No. It provides initial suggestions only. Eligibility depends on current scheme rules and your circumstances.

Can I save my favourite schemes?

Yes. Register and log in to add schemes to your Saved Schemes list.

Is AI Help connected to live AI?

This version uses built-in topic-based guidance. Live AI requires a provider API and secure backend configuration.

How can I protect my personal information?

Never share OTPs, passwords, UPI PINs or bank PINs. Use official portals for applications.
